Privacy Policy

How Plannix collects, uses and protects your information.

Home Privacy Policy

At Plannix, your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and how we keep it safe.

1. Information We Collect

  • Personal details you provide, such as your name, email address and mobile number
  • Booking information, including event type, date and location
  • Technical data such as IP address, browser type, device information and usage logs

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to:

  • Process event bookings and manage event services
  • Communicate with you regarding inquiries, confirmations and support
  • Improve our platform, services and user experience
  • Comply with legal and regulatory obligations

3. Information Sharing

Plannix does not sell or rent your personal information. We may share data only with:

  • Trusted event partners and vendors for service fulfilment
  • Payment gateways for secure transactions
  • Legal authorities if required by law

4. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure or destruction.

5. Cookies and Tracking

Our website may use cookies to enhance user experience, analyse website traffic and personalise content. You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access, update or delete your personal information
  • Withdraw consent where applicable
  • Request information about how your data is used

7. Third-Party Links

Plannix may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those sites.

8. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@plannix.app.
