About Plannix

A clearer path from occasion to celebration

Plannix brings occasion planning, package discovery, and provider comparison into one place — so you can choose with confidence and book with clarity.

How Plannix works

Every occasion deserves the right package. Plannix helps you find it — from first inspiration to confirmed booking.

Start with your occasion. Whether you're planning a wedding, birthday, corporate gathering, or an intimate celebration, Plannix organises packages by occasion so you can explore relevant options without searching blind.

Discover packages from providers. Browse curated offerings from event service providers — each package includes pricing, inclusions, capacity, and availability so you know exactly what you're comparing.

Compare and choose with clarity. Side-by-side, you can evaluate what fits your requirements and your budget. When you've found the right match, send a booking request directly to the provider.

Plannix is built for people who want a considered choice — not a rushed scroll through endless listings.
